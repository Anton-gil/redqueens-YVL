## 1. Exploit Summary

GoldAdapter.exchangeRate() derives its rate from the raw pool balance, so a direct token transfer into the pool inflates the rate without minting shares. A subsequent small deposit is credited at the inflated rate and mints rwaUSD far in excess of the collateral's fair value, leaving the protocol holding unbacked debt.
